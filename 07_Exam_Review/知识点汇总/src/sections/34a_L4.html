L4电子相关方法Electron correlation methods: CI · CC · MPn · BSSE · CBS

L4 补回 HF 丢掉的东西：先定义相关能、区分静态与动态相关；然后三大家族——组态相互作用（CI）、耦合簇（CC）、微扰理论（MPn），比较它们的变分性、大小一致性、标度和精度；最后是基组重叠误差（BSSE）与平衡校正、完备基组外推和各种"密度"。判断题最爱考"哪个方法变分 / 大小一致"。

§4.1 HF 回顾与相关能correlation energy

HF 的四个特征 [L4 p3]：① 平均场近似；② 单电子近似（每个自旋轨道描述一个电子的运动）；③ 多电子波函数是单电子 MO 的反对称化乘积（一个 Slater 行列式），满足 Pauli；④ HF 能量是 Hamiltonian 的期望值 $E_{HF}=\langle\Psi_{HF}|\hat H|\Psi_{HF}\rangle$，高于精确能量。

相关能 [L4 p3]{{ix:Correlation energy|相关能}} $$E_{corr}=E_{exact}-E_{HF}\ (<0)$$

严格说 $E_{HF}$ 应取 HF 极限（完备基组）；$E_{exact}$ 是精确非相对论能量。量级：约为总能量的 1%，但与化学键能同量级（He：−0.042 Ha ≈ −26 kcal/mol，{{p:sB.7}}）。

原句Electron correlation is, in effect, defined as inter-electron interactions that Hartree-Fock theory does not properly account for.电子相关 = HF 没有正确处理的电子间相互作用。[L4 p4]

§4.2 静态相关与动态相关static vs dynamic correlation

原句Static correlation refers to a situation where a single Slater determinant (i.e. a single population pattern of molecular orbitals) might not be a good approximation to the ground state. This often happens when the ground state is or becomes degenerate. … A famous example is the dissociation of the hydrogen molecule, where the restricted Hartree-Fock method produces a pair of ions instead of a pair of radicals.静态相关：一个行列式（一种轨道占据方式）不足以近似基态，常见于基态简并或变得简并时。例：H₂ 解离，RHF 给出一对离子而不是一对自由基。[L4 p4]
原句Dynamic correlation is the correlated motion of electrons resulting from the Coulomb repulsion. Being an average field theory, the HF method does not properly account for this effect.动态相关：库仑排斥导致的电子关联运动（瞬时互相避开）；HF 是平均场理论，处理不了。例：非弹性 X 射线散射不包含动态相关就描述不了。[L4 p4]

§4.4 变分原理在实践中：基组与性质variational principle in practice

原句In practice, the variational principle guarantees that a bigger basis set would yield a better approximation, though the law of diminishing returns certainly applies. Because of error compensation effects, the variational principle does not generally apply to properties other than energy, and even to energy differences … For property calculations, therefore, a bigger basis set does not necessarily mean a more accurate answer, but certainly means a longer processing time.更大基组 → 能量更好，但收益递减；由于误差抵消，变分原理不适用于能量以外的性质，甚至不适用于能量差；算性质时基组大不一定更准，但一定更慢。[L4 p5]{{ix:Error compensation|误差抵消}}

§4.5 组态相互作用（CI）configuration interaction

CI 波函数与久期方程 [L4 p2, p12–13]{{ix:Configuration interaction (CI)|组态相互作用}} $$\Psi=a_0\psi_{HF}+\sum_S a_S\psi_S+\sum_D a_D\psi_D+\sum_T a_T\psi_T+\cdots\qquad(\text{S/D/T = 单/双/三激发})$$ $$L=\langle\Psi|\hat H|\Psi\rangle-\lambda\left(\langle\Psi|\Psi\rangle-1\right),\quad \frac{\partial L}{\partial a_i}=0\ \Rightarrow\ (\mathbf H-E\mathbf I)\mathbf a=\mathbf 0\ \Leftrightarrow\ \mathbf{Ha}=E\mathbf a,\quad H_{ij}=\langle\psi_i|\hat H|\psi_j\rangle$$

在归一化约束下用 Lagrange 乘子最小化能量 → 普通本征值问题。线性变分：CI 系数由变分确定，MO 固定（取 HF 轨道）。

原句Exact wave function is obtained when ALL possible Slater Determinants are included in the wave function, while their individual weights are determined variationally … Such wave function is called: exact non-relativistic solution of the Schrodinger equation. … Such wave functions are only possible for very small molecules, mostly diatomics, with small basis sets … (FCI). In practice, it is common to impose a limit on the number of excited electrons compared to the ground state HF Slater Determinant, usually 2.FCI（在该基组内）= 精确非相对论解；实践中通常截断到双激发（CISD）。"The solution of this equation gives, in principle the exact answer. The only difficulty … is the very fast-growing size of the CI matrix." [L4 p2, p13]
性质
  • 截断 CI（CISD 等）是变分的（能量是上界），但不满足大小一致性（{{p:s4.8}}）。FCI 既变分又大小一致。
  • FCI 的精确是"给定基组内"的精确；基组不完备的误差仍在。
  • 标度：CID $O(n^5)$、CISD $O(n^6)$、CISDT $O(n^8)$、FCI $O(n!)$（{{p:s3.22}}）。

§4.6 行列式（SD）与组态函数（CSF）SD-CI and spin-adapted CI

原句Wave functions are usually characterized by a total spin value (S) … Significant computational savings can be obtained by combining SDs into spin-adapted Configuration State Functions (CSFs). States of different spin states do not mix by non-relativistic Hamiltonian: ⟨singlet|Ĥ|triplet⟩ = 0.把行列式组合成自旋匹配的 CSF（$\hat S^2$ 本征函数）可大幅节省；非相对论 Hamiltonian 不混合不同自旋态。[L4 p14]{{ix:Configuration state function (CSF)|组态函数}}
计数公式（n 个空间轨道、2n 个自旋轨道、N 个电子、总自旋 S）[L4 p14]{{ix:Weyl formula|Weyl 公式}} $$N_{SD}=\binom{2n}{N}=\frac{(2n)!}{N!\,(2n-N)!},\qquad N_{CSF}=\frac{2S+1}{n+1}\binom{n+1}{\frac N2-S}\binom{n+1}{\frac N2+S+1},\qquad N_{SD}\ge N_{CSF}$$

算好的例子（CAS(2,2)、CAS(4,4)、CAS(6,6)…）和核对方法见 {{p:sB.4}}。
